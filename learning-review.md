# Public Slovak A1 learning app

Includes the original 90-day course, English grammar explanations, original practice passages, 40 automatically checked listening drills and generated practice audio. Textbook conversations, their translations, textbook-specific audio, the PDF and scans are excluded.

Audio is synthetic. Progress is stored locally on each device. Microphone recordings are not uploaded. There has been no independent human language review, and the practice assessment is not a CEFR certification.
