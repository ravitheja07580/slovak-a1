Your Slovak app, on your phone.

Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. Open your published GitHub Pages link. No app sign-in is required.

Open the learning app

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

The Slovak A1 icon will open your learning path. You can also keep using the app directly in Safari.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Use Install Slovak A1 if it appears, or open Chrome’s menu and choose Add to Home screen / Install app.
  3. Follow the phone’s confirmation steps.

Audio, microphone and progress

Tap Play to start audio. For recording practice, allow microphone access when your browser asks. If recording is unavailable in an embedded browser, open the link directly in Safari or Chrome.

Keep an internet connection for lessons and recordings. Adding the app to your home screen does not download the entire course for offline use.

Progress stays on each device and browser; it does not sync automatically. Use Progress & backup to transfer the learning area’s drafts and scores. The 90-day course has its own backup in Settings. Recordings must be downloaded separately.
